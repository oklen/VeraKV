# Result status: what is officially verified vs locally run

| Status | Meaning | Which results |
|---|---|---|
| **Officially verified** | Our best run's per-question answers (`submissions/verakv_ama_submission.jsonl`) were re-judged by the AMA-Bench organizers and listed on the public leaderboard at **0.6478** — #1 as of 2026-07-15; a later entry (0.6975, dated 2026-09-09) has since overtaken it, so VeraKV ranks #2. | FLSA+FLSB (self-scored 0.6478) |
| **Local run of the official harness** | Full official `run.py` end-to-end (memory build → retrieval → answer → Qwen3-32B judge) on our GPUs, unmodified except the disclosed, config-gated answer-instruction swap. Raw per-question predictions + judge scores released for every tag. | `results/mu_merged_*.json` (index: `results/MANIFEST.md`) |
| **Official judge, our generation** | Answers generated by the KV-bridge (`kvmemory/kv_ama_bridge.py`, Qwen3-32B, fixed-K serving cell without overview) and scored by AMA-Bench's own judge (`ama/br_judge.py`, `ama/bg_judge.py`). A different protocol from the deployed pipeline — its text arm is a routed-text carrier baseline, never compared across protocols. | `results/oow/official_*.jsonl.gz` (Sec. 4 Table "oow", deployment map) |
| **Mechanism subset / probes** | Stripped HF harness on a fixed 103-episode within-window subset (n=824, Qwen3-8B unless stated, in-run judge), over-window cells (YaRN 4×), LOCOMO (n=1150), and synthetic register-tracking probes. Paired, deterministic; claims are paired contrasts, not absolute levels. | `results/oow/*.jsonl.gz`, `results/probes/` |
| **Microbenchmark** | Single-request prototype (KV-equivalence, side channel, efficiency, privacy, prefix-cache baseline) — parity/mechanism and cost claims only, not serving-system numbers. | `results/kv_equiv_*`, `results/kv_sidechannel/`, `results/kv_cost/`, `results/w5_privacy.json` |

Judge caveat (disclosed in the paper): the official docs report Qwen3-32B is a lenient judge in absolute terms;
all leaderboard comparisons share that judge. An independent Llama-3.1-70B re-judge of the identical
predictions of a full official-harness run scores it higher (0.677 vs 0.643, 84% item agreement;
`results/judge_llama70b/ama_rejudged.json`, `analysis/rejudge_llama70b.py`) — supportive, not definitive.

Reproduce: `scripts/run_ama.sh` (needs 8×A100 + the models staged; `ama/maxutil_run.sh` documents the
serving/launch recipe and its environment variables). CIs: `analysis/cluster_ci.py` over
`results/mu_merged_*.json`; paired contrasts on the KV runs: `analysis/oow_paired.py`.
